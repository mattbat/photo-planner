Plan a shoot: pick a family and who is coming, switch shot templates on and off (each shows how many shots it adds), and the shot list builds itself. Add your own shots, rename, star must-haves, remove and restore shots, get a suggested order with who to call in and who can leave, share the list as text, and duplicate a shoot. On the family screen, "How are they related?" tells you how two people are connected, and the tree now opens centred with a different colour for each branch.
