Shoot day: tap Start shoot and the shot list becomes a checklist. A big Done button moves to the next shot, Skip and Needs retake are beside it, and Undo takes back the last tap. See who is in each shot grouped by family branch, who to call in and who can leave next, and mark people as here or left so shots that need them wait. Add a shot on the spot, finish with a summary to share, and reopen a finished shoot. You can also pick a suggested pose for each shot and see them all on the Pose sheet.
