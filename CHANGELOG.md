# Changelog

What changed in each release of Photo Planner, in plain words. Newest first. The app shows the same notes under Settings → What’s new, and once after each update.

Changes that are merged but not released yet wait as notes in [`changes/`](changes/README.md). See "Releases" in the README for how a release is made.

## 0.4.1 (2026-11-16)

### Improved

- The Early/Late list is simpler: one row per person, grouped by household, no checkboxes.

### Fixed

- A change made right after adding something is no longer mistaken for an older copy when you restore a backup with Merge.

## 0.4.0 (2026-11-16)

### Improved

- Delete and replace questions now appear in a clean sheet with clear buttons.
- Shots for each household now come together in one run, after the grandparents and the cousins, so families aren't called back and forth.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-15)

### New

- Mark people as Early or Late for a shoot. Early people (an older guest, someone who needs a break soon) get their shots first; Late people (called away for a bit, a little one who needs a rest) wait until the end. You can set it before the shoot and on the day from Who's here, which re-orders only the shots still to do.
- When you link people in a family, you can now choose someone who is already in it instead of adding a new person. That makes it easy to join two branches you added separately. If a new name looks like someone already there, the app asks "Is this the same Emma?" and lets you link her instead.

### Improved

- The app and its help now use American spelling throughout: favorite, color, center, gray and so on.
- Full-group shots now come first by default, so you get them done while everyone is fresh. You can switch it off in Settings or on any shoot.
- Form fields line up and look consistent: the same height everywhere, dates and times side by side, and a divider between every row of people. The top bar and tab bar have proper icons, times read like "3:53 PM" everywhere, and shoots show a small progress ring.
- Pick several poses for a shot. Tap as many suggested poses as you like, put them in order, and on shoot day they show as a row you can swipe, with the directions one tap away.
- The Light section is simpler: it shows just the evening (or morning) around your shoot, with golden hour and sunset at a glance and how your shoot fits. Blue hour is gone. The sun direction now shows a little sun on the compass, with a caption saying when.
- Weather now shows °F and mph. If you prefer °C and km/h, switch to metric in Settings.

### Fixed

- "Shot 3 of 12" on the shoot day page now counts 1, 2, 3 all the way to the end, and tells you how many shots are left. It no longer jumps around when you skip, retake or pick a shot to do now.
- A planned shoot you've shared to your assistant can no longer be started on your phone by mistake. Tap Take back control first if you want to run it yourself.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-15)

### New

- A new app, Photo Planner, that installs on your Home Screen: Shoots, Families and Poses tabs, Settings with back up and restore, and light or dark to match your phone.
- Hand a shoot to your assistant. Tap Share shoot, then Text a link or AirDrop a file, and their phone opens the family, the date and the shot list, with your own poses (no photos, so the link stays small). One phone runs the checklist at a time: after you share a shoot that has started, your phone shows Shared with the other phone and locks until you share it back or tap Take back control. Send it back at the end and your copy has every check mark. A copy with fewer done shots than yours is never accepted by accident. On Shoots, Paste shoot and Open shoot file receive one.
- Add your clients' families: a guided Quick add, or load a demo family. Each family is drawn as a tree you can pinch, drag and tap to edit, with a List view, guests and pets, plain-words warnings if the relationships can't be right, and Share as a picture.
- Set a location for each shoot (your position, a search, typed coordinates or a pin on the map) and see golden hour on a colored timeline with your shoot drawn on it. Get a suggested start time, the sun direction with a compass, a 3-week date helper and the weather forecast, saved for when you are offline.
- A short intro the first time you open Photo Planner, ending in "Try the demo family" or "Add my own family". There is now a Help and guide in Settings, with pictures in light and dark: getting started, families and the tree, shot types, building the shot list, golden hour, shoot day, working with an assistant, poses, backups and common questions. Screens have a "?" link to the right page, and the order options now explain themselves. Shoot times in the Shoots list now match the rest of the app.
- The Poses tab now has a library of 60 illustrated poses, with words you can say to direct each one. Filter by category, group size and style, search, star your favorites, and add your own poses and example photos. It all works offline.
- Shoot day: tap Start shoot and the shot list becomes a checklist. A big Done button moves to the next shot, Skip and Needs retake are beside it, and Undo takes back the last tap. See who is in each shot grouped by family branch, who to call in and who can leave next, and mark people as here or left so shots that need them wait. Add a shot on the spot, finish with a summary to share, and reopen a finished shoot. You can also pick a suggested pose for each shot and see them all on the Pose sheet.
- Plan a shoot: pick a family and who is coming, switch shot templates on and off (each shows how many shots it adds), and the shot list builds itself. Add your own shots, rename, star must-haves, remove and restore shots, get a suggested order with who to call in and who can leave, share the list as text, and duplicate a shoot. On the family screen, "How are they related?" tells you how two people are connected, and the tree now opens centered with a different color for each branch.
